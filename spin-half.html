Measurement probabilities
AxisP(+)P(−)

P(±n) = (1 ± ⟨σₙ⟩)/2. For unit state direction s, the state surface is a sphere of radius ½ centered at s/2: |r − s/2| = ½. It touches the origin and reaches the state arrow's tip. A plane through the origin, perpendicular to the chosen measurement axis, divides its curved surface into the coloured ± parts. Each part's surface area ÷ total surface area equals its probability; no flat cut face or interior volume is counted. Mesh ratios approximate the exact areas.
